## Mood Mate project

Problem: People experience changing moods and may struggle to notice patterns or choose helpful activities. Mood Mate offers a simple private record and tailored, supportive suggestions. It is a self-reflection tool, not a diagnostic or emergency service.

Aim: Build and evaluate a small, usable application for recording mood entries, reviewing history, and receiving rule-based suggestions based on mood, intensity, energy and user preferences.

Target users: Adults who want a quick personal wellbeing journal, including students and people with varying daily routines. The interface should remain usable with keyboard and assistive technology. Do not claim clinical effectiveness.
